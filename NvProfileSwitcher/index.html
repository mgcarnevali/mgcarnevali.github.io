How it works

Configure your profiles once. NvProfileSwitcher handles the switching.

01

Create a profile

Choose a game or application and associate its executable with a profile.

02

Tune your colors

Adjust Digital Vibrance, hue, brightness, contrast and gamma for each display, with Live Preview.

03

Switch automatically

Bring the application to the foreground to activate its profile. Switch away and the appropriate profile returns.
